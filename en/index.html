隐私状态 · 音频留在本机

你的练习声音，只留在你的设备上

吉他工具（GuitarTool）隐私政策 · 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 8 日

无需账户音频不上传无广告与追踪

卫智超（以下简称“开发者”）重视你的隐私。本政策说明 GuitarTool 在调音、节拍练习、和弦查询、主屏幕小组件和 Apple Watch 功能中如何处理信息。

1. 我们不收集哪些信息

GuitarTool 不出售个人信息，也不将信息用于广告或追踪。本应用无需注册或登录，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话号码、精确位置、通讯录、照片或设备广告标识符，也不集成广告或第三方分析、追踪 SDK。开发者不收集或接收功能使用次数、使用时长等分析数据；用于系统评分提示的少量本地资格状态见第 4 节。

2. 麦克风与音频处理

iPhone、iPad 与 Apple Watch 上的调音器只有在你主动开始调音时才会请求并使用相应设备的麦克风权限。麦克风音频用于在当前设备上实时计算音高、音分、信号强度和稳定度。1.1.2 的手表调音器支持吉他、尤克里里、贝斯和半音阶模式；打开调音页面本身不会开始收音。

  • 音频完全在设备本地处理，不会发送到开发者或任何第三方服务器。
  • 麦克风录音不会保存到文件、相册、iCloud 或其他持久存储。
  • 停止调音、切换功能或应用进入后台后，实时采集会停止，音频处理缓冲会被释放；在 Apple Watch 上切换页面或离开 App 也会结束收音。
  • 你可以在 iOS/iPadOS 或 watchOS 系统设置中撤销 GuitarTool 的麦克风权限；撤销后对应设备的调音器无法使用。

3. 和弦卡片、照片与闪光灯

只有当你主动点击“保存到照片”时，应用才会请求照片“仅添加”权限，并把刚刚在设备上生成的和弦卡片写入系统照片图库。应用不会读取、浏览或上传你已有的照片和视频；拒绝权限不会影响调音、节拍器、和弦查询或系统分享。

启用节拍器闪光反馈时，应用只在前台且节拍器页面可见时控制设备闪光灯，不拍摄照片或视频，也不会读取相机画面。切换页面、进入后台或停止节拍器后，应用会请求立即关闭闪光灯。

4. 本地设置与 App Group

为了记住你的练习环境，应用会在设备上保存节拍器速度、拍号、训练方案（包括有声／静音小节、每段速度与小节数、循环设置）、调音预设、主题和界面语言等设置。部分节拍器状态通过 Apple App Group 在 GuitarTool 与其主屏幕小组件之间共享，以便小组件显示和控制同一套本地设置。

1.1.2 为避免在演奏中或过于频繁地请求评分，仅在本机保留有限的合格练习次数、练习日期、本地时区、用于去重的近期随机练习标识，以及已尝试提示的版本和时间。前台音频运行时长只用于判断一次练习是否满足条件，不保存完整练习时长日志。这些状态不与身份关联、不上传给开发者，不构成云端练习记录，也不记录你是否满意、给出的星级或评价内容。Apple 决定是否显示系统评分界面；提交评价由 Apple 处理。

这些设置保存在你的设备上；界面语言可同步至配对的 Apple Watch。删除应用通常也会删除其本地数据；系统备份行为由你的 iOS 与 iCloud 设置决定。

5. 网络、第三方服务与追踪

GuitarTool 的核心功能不需要网络连接。应用不包含第三方广告、分析或追踪 SDK，不创建用户画像，也不进行跨应用或跨网站追踪。

应用不提供向开发者上报的使用分析功能，也不显示分析授权提示；第 4 节所述评分资格仅在本机判断。

当前 1.1.2 的主题展厅包含七套完整主题（新增玄玉声场），主题及其图片均包含在安装包内，预览及已购主题的使用无需连接开发者的主题服务，也不需要另行下载素材。

App 的练习功能与四款基础主题免费。七套完整视觉主题仍分别通过 Apple App Store 应用内购买解锁；购买和恢复购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理，可能需要网络连接；开发者不接收银行卡信息，也不向自建主题服务发送交易凭据。Apple 对购买数据的处理见 App Store 与隐私。解锁后可离线使用主题。

官网、技术支持和本隐私政策网页由 GitHub Pages 托管；访问时，托管方会处理提供网页所需的网络请求信息，其处理方式见 GitHub 隐私声明。本隐私政策网页本身不加载外部字体、分析脚本或追踪像素，不设置 Cookie。语言切换打开对应语言的静态页面，不保存语言偏好。

6. 儿童隐私

GuitarTool 不要求儿童或其他用户提交个人资料。由于应用不提供账户、社交或数据上传功能，儿童使用时不会因此向开发者提交个人资料。

7. 数据安全与你的选择

最小化数据是本应用的主要保护方式：音频不上传、不留存，练习设置只保存在 Apple 平台提供的本地存储中，开发者不接收使用分析数据。你可以通过系统设置管理麦克风和照片权限，或删除应用及其本地数据。

8. 政策更新

本次更新补充 1.1.2 的训练设置、系统评分资格和七套主题说明。如果应用的数据处理方式发生变化，本页面会更新日期并说明新的处理方式。涉及新数据收集或新权限时，将在功能启用前提供清楚说明，并按适用规则取得许可。

Privacy status · Audio stays local

Your practice audio stays on your device

GuitarTool Privacy Policy · Updated October 8, 2026

No accountNo audio uploadsNo ads or tracking

Zhichao Wei (the “developer”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how GuitarTool handles information across tuning, metronome practice, chord lookup, Home Screen widgets, and Apple Watch features.

1. Information we do not collect

GuitarTool does not sell personal information or use it for advertising or tracking. The app requires no account or sign-in. It does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and it contains no advertising or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The developer does not collect or receive feature-use counts, time spent, or other usage analytics. The small amount of local state used for system rating eligibility is described in section 4.

2. Microphone and audio processing

On iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, the tuner requests and uses that device's microphone only after you choose to start tuning. Audio is used on that device to calculate pitch, cents, signal strength and stability in real time. The 1.1.2 Watch tuner offers guitar, ukulele, bass and chromatic modes; opening its page alone does not start capture.

  • Audio is processed entirely on your device and is not sent to the developer or any third-party server.
  • Microphone recordings are not saved to files, Photos, iCloud, or other persistent storage.
  • Live capture stops and analysis buffers are released when you stop tuning, switch tools or move the app to the background. On Apple Watch, changing pages or leaving the app also ends capture.
  • You can revoke GuitarTool microphone access in iOS/iPadOS or watchOS Settings at any time. The tuner on that device will then be unavailable.

3. Chord cards, Photos, and torch feedback

The app requests add-only Photos access only after you choose “Save to Photos,” and writes the chord card that was just generated on your device to the system photo library. The app does not read, browse, or upload your existing photos or videos. Denying access does not affect tuning, the metronome, chord lookup, or system sharing.

When metronome torch feedback is enabled, the app controls the device torch only while the app is in the foreground and the metronome page is visible. It does not capture photos, video, or camera frames. The app requests that the torch turn off when you switch pages, enter the background, or stop the metronome.

4. Local settings and App Group sharing

The app stores settings such as tempo, time signature, practice presets (including audible/silent bar counts, tempo stages, bars per stage and loop settings), tuning presets, theme, and interface language on your device. Some metronome state is shared through an Apple App Group between GuitarTool and its Home Screen widget so the widget can display and control the same local configuration.

To avoid requesting a rating during playing or too often, version 1.1.2 keeps limited eligibility state on-device: a qualifying-practice counter, practice dates, local time zone, recent random practice IDs for deduplication, and versions and times of previous prompt attempts. Foreground audio duration is used only to qualify a practice; a full duration log is not saved. This state is not linked to an identity or uploaded to the developer, is not a cloud practice history, and does not record satisfaction, stars or review text. Apple decides whether to display the system rating interface and handles any review you submit.

These settings stay on your devices; the interface language may sync to your paired Apple Watch. Deleting the app normally removes its local data. System backup behavior depends on your iOS and iCloud settings.

5. Network use, third parties, and tracking

GuitarTool's core features do not require an internet connection. The app includes no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, creates no user profile, and performs no cross-app or cross-site tracking.

The app has no developer-facing usage analytics feature or analytics consent prompt. Rating eligibility described in section 4 is evaluated only on your device.

The current 1.1.2 release bundles seven complete themes and their images, including the new Jade Resonance theme. Previewing and using purchased themes require no connection to the developer's theme service or separate asset download.

All practice features and four basic looks are free. The seven complete visual themes remain separate Apple App Store in-app purchases. Apple StoreKit handles purchases and restores, which may require an internet connection; the developer does not receive card details or send transaction credentials to a developer-operated theme service. See Apple’s App Store & Privacy for Apple’s handling of purchase data. Once unlocked, the themes work offline.

The website, support pages and this policy are hosted on GitHub Pages. The host processes network request information to serve these pages; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. This privacy policy page does not load external fonts, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels and does not set cookies. The language links open the corresponding static page without saving a language preference.

6. Children's privacy

GuitarTool does not ask children or other users to submit personal profiles. The app has no accounts, social features, or data-upload feature through which a child could submit profile information to the developer.

7. Security and your choices

Data minimization is the app's primary protection: audio is neither uploaded nor retained, practice settings remain in storage provided by Apple platforms, and the developer receives no usage analytics. You can manage microphone and Photos access in system settings, or delete the app and its local data.

8. Changes to this policy

This update explains 1.1.2 training settings, system rating eligibility and seven themes. If the app's data practices change, this page will show a revised date and describe the new handling. If a feature introduces new data collection or permissions, the app will explain them before the feature is enabled and request consent where required.
