Your practice audio stays on your device

GuitarTool 1.1.4 Privacy Policy · Updated October 20, 2026 · New analytics terms apply when version 1.1.4 first becomes available to users

No accountNo audio uploadsAnalytics is your choice

Zhichao Wei (the “developer”) respects your privacy. This policy covers GuitarTool for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and its companion websites. It explains information handling for tuning, metronome practice, voice counting, chord lookup and Home Screen widgets. The App Store lists Shanghai Qishan Cultural Communication Co., Ltd. as the seller. Android and Wear OS are covered by the separate Android privacy policy.

Information scope and your choice

GuitarTool does not sell personal information or use it for advertising or cross-app/cross-site tracking. No account or sign-in is required. Usage analytics does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, recordings or precise pitch measurements. Information you voluntarily send to support is explained under “Contact the developer.”

iPhone and iPad offer optional “Help improve GuitarTool” analytics, off by default. The official PostHog SDK initializes and sends events only after you read the notice and explicitly agree, and only when that build has valid project configuration. Declining does not affect tuning, metronome practice, chords, themes or purchases. Apple Watch and Home Screen widgets do not initialize the SDK or independently send analytics events in this phase.

Version scope: This page explains information handling in version 1.1.4. Its optional analytics terms apply when that version first becomes available to users, and collection starts only after your explicit consent. The published version 1.1.3 contains no third-party analytics SDK and sends no feature-usage analytics to the developer. Updating this policy does not enable collection in version 1.1.3.

Microphone and audio processing

On iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, the tuner requests and uses that device's microphone only after you choose to start tuning. Audio is used on that device to calculate pitch, cents, signal strength and stability in real time. The Watch tuner offers guitar, ukulele, bass and chromatic modes; opening its page alone does not start capture.

  • Audio is processed entirely on your device and is not sent to the developer or any third-party server.
  • Microphone recordings are not saved to files, Photos, iCloud, or other persistent storage.
  • Live capture stops and analysis buffers are released when you stop tuning, switch tools or move the app to the background. On Apple Watch, changing pages or leaving the app also ends capture.
  • You can revoke GuitarTool microphone access in iOS/iPadOS or watchOS Settings at any time. The tuner on that device will then be unavailable.

Voice counting and previews

Version 1.1.3 adds the “Voice” sound to the iPhone and iPad metronome. Prerecorded Chinese counts from one through twelve and their speed variants are bundled as WAV files. Counting, the one-to-four preview at your current BPM when selecting the sound, and sample selection and playback for tempo stages all run on your device. The app does not download speech, call a runtime AI, speech-synthesis or cloud service, capture your voice, or request microphone or speech-recognition permission for this feature. Changing the interface language does not translate these built-in Chinese counts. This feature is not available on Apple Watch.

Chord cards, Photos, and torch feedback

The app requests add-only Photos access only after you choose “Save to Photos,” and writes the chord card that was just generated on your device to the system photo library. The app does not read, browse, or upload your existing photos or videos. Denying access does not affect tuning, the metronome, chord lookup, or system sharing.

When metronome torch feedback is enabled, the app controls the device torch only while the app is in the foreground and the metronome page is visible. It does not capture photos, video, or camera frames. The app requests that the torch turn off when you switch pages, enter the background, or stop the metronome.

Local settings and App Group sharing

The app stores settings such as tempo, time signature, sound selection (including Voice), practice settings (including audible/silent bar counts, tempo stages, bars per stage and loop settings), custom presets, tuning presets, theme and interface language on your device. Sound selection can be saved in a local custom practice preset; the app does not save recordings of your voice. Some metronome state is shared through an Apple App Group between GuitarTool and its Home Screen widget so the widget can display and control the same local configuration.

To avoid requesting a rating during playing or too often, the rating module keeps limited eligibility state on-device: a qualifying-practice counter, practice dates, local time zone, recent random practice IDs for deduplication, and versions and times of previous prompt attempts. Within that module, foreground audio duration is used only to qualify a practice; a full duration log is not saved. This rating state is not linked to an identity or uploaded to the developer, is not a cloud practice history, and does not record satisfaction, stars or review text. Aggregate usage durations sent with your consent to optional analytics are explained in the next section. Apple decides whether to display the system rating interface and handles any review you submit.

These settings stay on your devices; the interface language may sync to your paired Apple Watch. Deleting the app normally removes its local data. System backup behavior depends on your iOS and iCloud settings.

Network use, PostHog and third parties

Core practice features work offline. With your consent, PostHog Cloud receives limited product events for the developer to understand tool operation, practice use, return use and theme purchase flows and improve the product. After consent, the SDK may also request service configuration. Analytics is not used for advertising. Automatic page/click/lifecycle collection, session replay, remote feature flags, person-profile processing and IP-based geolocation are disabled.

  • Device ID (random installation ID) and event time: associate activity across time on the same installation without linking an account or another device. This is not your name, but it still links usage records and must not be described as fully anonymous.
  • Product interaction: tool/page categories, starts/completions/failures, ending reasons, aggregate duration, instrument/tuning preset, tempo and training mode, chord view/preview/share actions, and theme previews and application. No per-beat or per-frame data, audio, precise pitches, free text or shared content is uploaded.
  • Purchase history: theme/product identifiers, purchase attempts and results such as success, cancellation, pending approval or failure, and separate purchase-restoration results. No receipts, transaction IDs, purchase tokens, card details or payment amounts are uploaded.
  • Performance data: aggregate elapsed time for tool-start attempts and product queries, used to understand operation responsiveness.
  • Other diagnostic data: limited fixed error codes for audio startup/recovery, product loading, purchases and related flows, used to understand functional problems. Crash logs and free-form error details are not uploaded.

Environment sent with events: platform, app version/build, OS version, phone/tablet type, interface language/locale and analytics environment. Random tool-run and purchase/restore-attempt IDs link events belonging to one operation. SDK delivery also uses technical fields such as event deduplication IDs, sessions and SDK version.

All five data types above are used for product analytics and linked across use of the same installation through its random installation ID. They are not used for advertising, personalized recommendations or cross-app/cross-site tracking.

Provider: PostHog, Inc.; website; privacy policy and contact information. Events travel over encrypted connections to a PostHog Cloud US-region project and are stored in the United States. Data from mainland China is transferred outside China. Disabling GeoIP prevents IP-derived location properties; PostHog and network infrastructure still process source IP addresses to receive requests, secure and operate the service. It does not mean that network transmission involves no IP information.

Events may be buffered locally and sent in batches or retried when connectivity returns. You may switch analytics off in settings at any time: collection stops and local pending events and analytics identity are cleared. Opting in again creates a new random ID. Turning analytics off does not automatically erase data already received in the cloud, and a request already in transit may have arrived. Save the current analytics ID if you wish to request access or deletion using the contact email below. If that ID is cleared and you have not retained it, we may be unable to match historical records to you.

Retention and deletion: analytics events are retained for no longer than one year from collection and deleted when that period expires. You may also request earlier deletion. View and save your current analytics ID in the App's “Help improve GuitarTool” settings, then email that ID and your deletion request to weizhichao1027@gmail.com. The developer will use the ID to locate and process deletion of the associated analytics records. Turning analytics off does not automatically delete uploaded records, so save the ID before switching it off. This retention period covers analytics events; audio, local settings, support emails and Apple transaction records are handled under their respective sections of this policy.

Theme artwork and voice samples are bundled. Apple StoreKit handles purchases/restores and may need a network connection. The developer does not receive card details or send transaction credentials to a self-hosted theme backend. Optional analytics records only the product and outcome described above and does not replace Apple's transaction records. See App Store & Privacy.

GitHub Pages hosts the website, support and policy pages and processes network information needed to serve them; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. This policy page uses no external fonts, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or cookies. The website tuner requests microphone access only after you start it, analyzes pitch locally in the browser and neither stores nor uploads recordings. Capture stops when you stop, leave or hide the page. The web metronome generates sound locally without microphone access. External services handle information under their own policies.

Children's privacy

The app does not require children to provide accounts or personal profiles, and analytics is off by default. We do not knowingly collect children's analytics without parental consent where required. Parents should manage analytics choices on the device. Contact the email below if you believe a child's data has been collected improperly so we can investigate and handle a deletion request.

Security and your choices

Protection includes analytics off by default, explicit consent, limited event fields, encrypted transport and restricted analytics access. Audio is neither uploaded nor retained; local rating eligibility, custom practice preset names and complete training plans are not uploaded as analytics events. Optional analytics includes only the limited configuration fields listed above, such as tempo and training mode. Manage analytics in app settings and microphone/Photos access in system settings. Deleting the app normally removes local data but does not erase analytics already received in the cloud, cards you shared or Apple's transaction records.
