GuitarTool Android 隐私政策

本政策适用于 GuitarTool Android（包名 com.guitar123.toolbox）及配套 Wear OS 应用。开发者：Qishan Labs。更新日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日。

音频与权限

调音器仅在用户开启调音、并授权麦克风后读取音频，音高计算在设备本地进行。应用不保存音频文件，不将录音发送到开发者服务器。退出调音页面或应用进入后台时，手机调音停止；Wear OS 在进入环境模式时暂停，恢复交互模式后可继续用户已开启的调音。相机权限仅用于闪光灯随节拍闪烁，不拍照或录像。通知权限用于后台节拍播放通知。相册保存功能在较新 Android 版本中仅写入用户选择保存的和弦图片；旧版本需要存储写入权限。拒绝权限后仍可使用不依赖该权限的功能。

本地数据

主题选择、语言、节拍设置、练习预设、调音设置及购买凭证保存在本设备。自动评价时机仅使用本地的练习完成计数、练习日期、版本与请求时间，不上传上述评价计数。应用没有开发者账户或广告 SDK。手机和平板版本中的 PostHog 使用分析已禁用；Wear OS 不集成分析 SDK。卸载应用会移除其本地应用数据；已导出到相册或主动分享的图片需由用户在对应应用中管理。

产品使用分析暂不启用

本版不提供使用分析入口，不初始化 PostHog SDK，不采集或发送功能使用、练习时长、购买结果或诊断分析事件。已有的同意记录和项目配置也不能恢复采集；本机遗留的待发送分析队列会在启动清理时移除。手表和小组件不独立发送分析事件。未来如提供分析功能，我们会先落实数据保留和删除安排，更新政策，并在采集前提供说明和取得所需同意。

如果你曾使用明确开启分析的测试版本，已上传至 PostHog 美国区的数据不会因安装本版、关闭分析或卸载而自动删除。可将此前保存的分析标识和请求发送至下方支持邮箱，开发者将核实记录并通过 PostHog 支持安排删除；删除为异步处理，完成情况需要核验。没有保留标识时可能无法匹配历史记录。本次调整不表示历史数据已被删除，也不改变已对既有数据作出的承诺。服务方信息见 PostHog 隐私政策。

Google Play 与设备配对

主题购买和恢复购买通过 Google Play Billing 完成。Google 根据其政策处理支付和购买信息，开发者应用验证签名购买凭证以判断主题使用权。应用可通过 Google Play 请求原生评价，是否展示由 Google 决定。配对的手机与手表通过 Google Wearable Data Layer 同步应用语言设置。应用不向开发者服务器上传音频或和弦内容。Google 服务的数据处理受 Google 隐私政策约束。

用户主动分享与联系

分享和弦会将生成的图片交给用户选择的分享应用；分享应用链接同样由用户选择接收渠道。反馈功能打开邮件应用，只有用户主动发送后，开发者才会收到邮件内容和发件信息；请不要发送不必要的敏感信息。如需了解、删除主动发送的反馈信息，请联系 weizhichao1027@gmail.com。

保留、删除与网站访问

本地设置和练习数据保留至你在应用内重置对应内容、清除应用数据或卸载应用。购买凭证可随恢复购买重新取得；清除本地数据不会删除 Google Play 的交易记录。主动发送的支持邮件仅用于处理请求、后续支持及适用的记录义务，并仅在这些目的所需期间保留；可通过上述邮箱申请访问、更正或删除。分享后的内容由你选择的平台按其规则管理。

公开隐私页面托管于 GitHub Pages。访问网页时，托管方可能处理 IP 地址、请求和浏览器信息以提供并保护网站，具体见 GitHub 隐私声明。本页面不嵌入广告、分析脚本、跟踪像素或第三方字体。应用内离线副本无需访问托管网站。

政策更新

如未来功能或数据处理方式发生变化，我们将更新本政策，并在适用时提供提示或征得授权。

GuitarTool Android Privacy Policy

This policy covers GuitarTool Android (com.guitar123.toolbox) and its companion Wear OS app. Developer: Qishan Labs. Updated October 21, 2026.

The tuner processes microphone audio locally only after you start tuning and grant permission. The app does not save recordings or upload audio to the developer. Phone tuning stops when leaving the tuner or backgrounding the app. Wear OS pauses tuning in ambient mode and can resume an existing user request when interactive again. Camera permission controls only the metronome flashlight; no photos or video are captured. Notifications support background metronome playback. Saving a chord image writes the image you choose to save; older Android versions require storage permission.

Preferences, practice presets, tuning settings and signed purchase receipts remain on your device. Local practice completion counts, dates, app versions and review request times determine when a native review request may be made. There are no developer accounts or advertising SDKs. PostHog analytics is disabled in the phone/tablet app. The Wear OS app contains no analytics SDK. Uninstalling removes app data; images you export or share remain in the destination you choose.

Usage analytics is disabled

This version provides no analytics opt-in control, does not initialize the PostHog SDK, and does not collect or send feature-usage, practice-duration, purchase-result or diagnostic analytics events. Previous consent or existing project configuration cannot resume collection; leftover local pending analytics queues are removed during startup cleanup. Watch and widget surfaces do not independently send analytics events. Before introducing analytics in a future release, we will establish retention and deletion arrangements, update this policy, and provide notice and obtain the required consent before collection.

If you previously used a test build with analytics explicitly enabled, data already uploaded to PostHog in the United States is not automatically deleted by installing this version, turning analytics off or uninstalling. Send your previously saved analytics ID and request to the support email below. The developer will verify the records and arrange deletion through PostHog support. Deletion is asynchronous and completion must be checked. Without a retained ID, historical records may not be identifiable. This change does not mean historical data has been deleted and does not change commitments already made about existing data. See the PostHog privacy policy for provider information.

Google Play Billing handles purchases and restoration. The app verifies signed receipts to determine theme access. Google controls billing and whether its native review prompt appears. Paired devices use Google Wearable Data Layer to synchronize the app language preference. Google's processing is governed by its privacy policy.

Sharing hands a generated image or app link to your chosen app. Feedback opens your email app; the developer receives your message and sender information only if you send it. To ask about or request deletion of feedback you sent, contact weizhichao1027@gmail.com. Support emails are used to handle your request, provide follow-up support and meet applicable record-keeping obligations, and retained only as needed for those purposes. You may request access, correction or deletion using that address.

Local settings and practice data remain until you reset the relevant content, clear app data or uninstall. Restoring purchases may retrieve receipts again; deleting local data does not delete Google Play transaction records. Content you share remains subject to your chosen destination’s controls.

The public policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. The host may process IP addresses, requests and browser information to operate and protect the website, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page embeds no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or third-party fonts. The bundled offline policy does not contact the website.

We will update this policy if app functionality or data handling changes.
