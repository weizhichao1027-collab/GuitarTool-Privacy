Why foreground media playback is needed

The metronome produces a continuous, user-requested beat for music practice. Users need the beat to continue while viewing sheet music in another app or while the screen is off. Interrupting or deferring playback breaks the practice tempo. Playback begins only after the user's explicit Start action and can be stopped in the app or its ongoing notification.

The declared permission is FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK. The tuner does not use this service to record in the background.
